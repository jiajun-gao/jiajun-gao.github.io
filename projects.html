Jarvis

Featured · ECE 445 Senior Design
Spring 2026

A desktop robot that talks, emotes, and knows where the table ends.

  • ECE 445 Hall of Fame
  • Grainger Engineering Senior Design Competition finalist
  • Team lead · 3 people

Jarvis is an ESP32-S3 companion robot that holds a spoken conversation, shows expressions on an LCD face, and drives around a desk. I came up with the concept, designed the end-to-end architecture, and built the AI and software pipeline.

Design rule

The language model never drives the motors. It can only ask. The ESP32 decides, and the obstacle and cliff sensors can veto any request.

What I built

  • The voice pipeline: offline wake-word detection, WebSocket audio streaming, VAD and ASR, Gemini 2.5 Flash responses, streamed Edge TTS, and MCP-based robot actions.
  • The safety layer: ESP32 arbitration, IR obstacle and cliff sensing, motion vetoes, and separate AI and browser-control modes.
  • Integration and bring-up across the Face and Body PCBs, LCD expressions, audio, Wi-Fi, motor drivers, battery power, and sensors.

Retrieval-augmented QA under knowledge edits

A RAG pipeline for multi-hop question answering over a counterfactual knowledge base of 371 edited facts. BM25 and dense Qwen3-Embedding retrievers feed a Qwen2.5-3B generator.

  • Cosine-similarity and BM25 retrieval with deterministic top-k ranking that stays the same when the corpus is reordered.
  • A structured-output prompt raised answer accuracy 3.7× with zero JSON parse failures across 568 generations.
  • Compared context sizes of k = 0, 1, 4, and 8, used gold-evidence (oracle) runs to diagnose failures, and tested robustness to fact edits and answer-order changes.
