Gradient penalty versus weight clipping for Wasserstein GANs.

I led a summer research project supervised by Prof. Mark Vogelsberger (MIT) that compared the two standard ways of enforcing the Lipschitz constraint in a Wasserstein GAN. I implemented WGAN-GP and WGAN-CP in PyTorch and measured training stability, convergence, FID, and Inception Score on CIFAR-10 and Fashion-MNIST.

Finding
WGAN-GP produced better images at a higher computational cost
Datasets
CIFAR-10, Fashion-MNIST
Metrics
FID, Inception Score, stability, convergence
